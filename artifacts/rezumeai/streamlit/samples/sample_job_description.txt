Product Analyst — Atlas

Atlas is looking for a product analyst to help teams make better decisions using data. You will partner with product, engineering, design, and marketing to define metrics, develop experiments, and communicate findings.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze product usage and customer retention with SQL and Python.
- Design and evaluate A/B tests and communicate statistical results.
- Build dashboards in Tableau or a similar BI tool.
- Develop reliable data pipelines and improve data quality.
- Present recommendations to product and executive stakeholders.

Qualifications:
- 3+ years of product analytics or data analysis experience.
- Strong SQL, Python, and statistics skills.
- Experience with experimentation, retention, and activation metrics.
- Clear written communication and cross-functional collaboration.
- Experience with BigQuery, Tableau, or similar platforms is a plus.
